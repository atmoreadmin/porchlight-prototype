How cash buyers price your home

No black box. These are the two rules most investors use. Our pricing step runs them on your home.

House flippers: the 70% rule

70% × after-repair value − repairs = max offer

The other 30% covers their selling costs, loan costs, holding time and profit.

Landlords: the 1% rule

Monthly rent ÷ 1% − repairs = max offer

Rent should be at least 1% of their total cost each month so it covers the mortgage, taxes and upkeep.
